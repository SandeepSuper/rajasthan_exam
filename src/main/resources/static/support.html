Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I buy a premium exam?
A: Go to the "Home" or "Community" section, select a premium exam/category, and complete the secure Razorpay payment.

Q: My app is offline, what do I do?
A: Ensure you have an active internet connection. Downloaded tests can be taken offline via the "Downloads" section in your Profile.

Q: How to reset my password?
A: Use the "Forgot Password" option on the Login screen.
